Final-year project report

PV-based Single-Input Multiple-Output Two-Phase Interleaved DC-DC Converter

B.E. EEE (Marine)AMET University202334 slides
Download PDF Open original PDF
Summary

A SEPIC-Ćuk two-phase interleaved converter that takes a 150 V solar PV input and supplies a battery-charging port, a DC motor, and an inverter-fed AC load. The phases are switched 180° apart so their ripple currents cancel. It was simulated in MATLAB/Simulink and built in hardware around a PIC16F877A, IRF840 MOSFETs and an IR2110 driver.

GuideDr. T. Baldwin Immanuel, Assoc. Prof., EEE

Outputs2 × DC + 1 × AC (inverter)
